About Us

Human Free Light (HFL) is a civic organization advocating full personal freedom and building an alternative governance system. Our mission is to liberate humanity by empowering individuals to operate independently of centralized control. Once objectives are achieved and territory secured, HFL will self-dissolve to ensure autonomy remains with citizens.
